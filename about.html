Our Mission

Healthier and happier, one sip and bite at a time.

That's the mission Gridiron Cafe was built on — real coffee, real food, made to actually be good for you, without making a big deal out of it. Healthy shakes, healthy teas, healthy coffee, healthy food: not as a lecture, just as what's on the menu every day.

The Story

From Gridiron Fitness to Gridiron Cafe.

Gridiron Cafe grew out of Gridiron Fitness, a gym built from the ground up over the past several years. What started as a place to work out became a place people wanted to eat well too — so the cafe opened in 2022 as its own thing: a coffee and food counter for the whole community, not just gym members. Today Gridiron Cafe has two locations, in Crystal City and Carrizo Springs, both serving the same menu and carrying the same mission the gym started with.

Meet Jay

The owner behind it all.

Jay is the owner of Gridiron Fitness and Gridiron Cafe — Crystal City & Carrizo Springs. Building Gridiron Fitness meant helping change how the town thought about health; Gridiron Cafe is the next chapter of that same idea, in coffee and food form.
