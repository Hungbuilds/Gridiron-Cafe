This Privacy Policy explains how Gridiron Cafe collects and uses information through this website.

Information We Collect

If the Site includes an online ordering system, contact form, or newsletter signup, we may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email, phone number, and order details. We do not knowingly collect information beyond what's needed to fulfill an order or respond to a message.

How We Use It

  • To process and fulfill online orders
  • To respond to questions or messages sent through the Site
  • To improve the Site and our service

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies & Analytics

The Site may use basic analytics tools to understand how visitors use it (for example, which pages are viewed most). This helps us improve the Site over time. You can disable cookies through your browser settings if you prefer.

Third-Party Links

This Site links to third-party platforms, including Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, and any online ordering provider we use. Those platforms have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them separately — we aren't responsible for how they handle your information.

Data Retention

We retain information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected — for example, order records or message history.

Your Choices

You can contact either location directly to ask what information we have on file or to request it be removed, where applicable.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page.
